Cyber security challenges continue to evolve rapidly, driven by advances in artificial intelligence, cloud technologies, digital transformation, and increasingly sophisticated threat actors. Addressing these challenges requires stronger collaboration between academia, industry, policymakers, and practitioners.
This interactive workshop will bring together researchers, cyber security professionals, industry leaders, policymakers, and students to explore emerging cyber security threats, identify future research priorities, and discuss opportunities for collaboration. Participants will engage in a series of facilitated discussions, breakout activities, and networking sessions focused on cyber resilience, responsible AI, skills development, security-by-design, and the future cyber workforce.
The workshop aims to generate practical recommendations for strengthening partnerships between universities and industry while supporting the development of a more resilient and innovative cyber security ecosystem within the United Kingdom.
