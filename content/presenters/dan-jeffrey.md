Dan is a proven national level Cyber, Technology, Privacy, and Operations leader who led the transformation of the NHS’ Cyber Security capability and posture in the wake of the WannaCry attack ahead of leading the Cyber Security delivery and operationalisation response during the COVID19 pandemic. In addition, Dan has delivered transformational strategies, architectures, and operating models to clients across the UK Public sector and Internationally. Dan is a Professor in Practice and Cyber Leader in Residence at the University of Lancaster where he teaches on the NCSC Accredited Masters and NCSC Cyber Executive MBA courses. Dan is also Technical Committee Member for the Cyber Monitoring Centre and previously he was the Chair of the Association of Blood Operators Security Group.
