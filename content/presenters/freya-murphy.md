Freya is a final-year PhD student at the Surrey Centre for Cyber Security, focusing on formal methods and verification for cybersecurity. She is interested in bridging the gap between theory and real-world impact and evaluating the security of computer systems within a broader context. Currently, she splits her time between Heriot-Watt University and the University of Surrey. In 2026, she spent two months at the University of Luxembourg SaToSS group, funded by the Turing Scheme.
